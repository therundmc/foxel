# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Laser pointer: move the mouse quickly in the view and the fox chases the cursor and pounces on it.
- Boop: click the fox's head to make it squint and sneeze.
- Treats: new **Give a Treat** command and view title button; the fox runs to the treat and eats it.
- Coat colours: new `foxel.coat` setting with red, arctic, silver and fennec.

## 1.0.0

- First release: a pixel-art fox cub companion that wanders, naps, hunts butterflies, plays fetch, can be petted and reacts to saves, errors, tasks and debugging.
