# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Touch: the fox reacts to where you click it, with three different reactions each for the nose, head, back, paws and tail (blep, nuzzle, head tilt, play bow, high five, twirl, startled jump, puffed-up tail and more). Clicking again does not restart the reaction; keep clicking and it gets the zoomies.
- Petting now needs the mouse button held down while stroking; just moving the mouse over the fox no longer pets it.
- Eye tracking: the fox follows the cursor with its eyes and turns around if you stay behind it.
- Intro: every time the Foxel view appears, the fox pokes its head in from the edge, looks around, ducks back shyly, then trots in to say hello.
- Ball tricks: after bringing the ball back, the fox sometimes balances it on its nose like a seal, tosses it up and catches it, or rolls it between its paws.
- Dreamy naps: the sleeping fox breathes slowly under rising Zzz, twitches its ears and tail, sticks its tongue out and dreams of the ball, a treat or a butterfly.
- Bouncier, cuter run.
- Plays on its own with a ball lying next to it.
- Treats: new **Give a Treat** command and view title button. A bone falls and the fox lies down to eat it bite by bite. Grab the bone with the mouse to hand-feed it: the fox sits and begs, and takes it once you hold it to its mouth.
- Coat colours: new `foxel.coat` setting with red, arctic, silver and fennec.

## 1.0.0

- First release: a pixel-art fox cub companion that wanders, naps, hunts butterflies, plays fetch, can be petted and reacts to saves, errors, tasks and debugging.
